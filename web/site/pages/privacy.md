# Privacy

This site sets no cookies and collects no personal data: it only counts how many people visit and what they read.

## What is counted

Page views, clicks on the site's links and buttons, and commands copied from its pages are counted with Umami Cloud, a service that stores no IP address and no personal data about visitors.

## What is not

Do Not Track is honoured: if your browser sends it, nothing is counted. The app under `/app/` isn't measured at all.
