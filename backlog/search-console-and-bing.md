Search engines find a new site faster, and report what they index, once its owner registers it. ChatGPT's search draws in part on Bing's index, so Bing matters as much as Google here. Only the operator can see these reports. Nothing in the build waits on this.

`clm-app.dev` is registered with Google Search Console (verified by DNS TXT record) and Bing Webmaster Tools, and both have `https://clm-app.dev/sitemap.xml`.

1. A week after the release, check both report the homepage and the guide's pages as indexed, and delete this item.
