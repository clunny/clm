---
title: "Removing behaviour always needs the human's yes"
date: 2026-11-13
status: accepted
---

A scenario, a requirement, a capability or an architectural constraint that states behaviour is never dropped without the human's yes. The re-founding of 2026-10-29 lost behaviour through deletions nobody read, and `no-dead-code` removes whatever serves no written rule, so a silent cut in the spec becomes a silent cut in the product.

The human reads no pass over what is kept or added; their only pass is over cuts. The AI proposes cuts and never drops, or carries, a burden in silence: a session or a check that sees something should probably go says what it is, the evidence, what would be lost in plain words and what it would save. A keep is recorded with what it was decided against, and the cut isn't proposed again until that evidence changes.

It is a carried `method` constraint, labelled a principle: no program yet tells a removal of behaviour from a rewording. The gate can see a removed requirement, a requirement with fewer scenarios and a deleted test, but not whether a merged scenario still states what two did, and which of those makes a branch need review is open in the plan. Until it is settled, a session that removes behaviour lists what it removes and asks, and a branch that deletes tests lists them for the human to read before the land.
